Inspired by the warmth of old Soviet-era Nixie tubes and Japanese VFD displays.
The goal is to drive these with modern microcontrollers while keeping the aesthetic analog and imperfect.

## what's happening

Building a driver board that talks I2C so any microcontroller can spin up a display without dealing with the high-voltage side.

Reference videos that sparked this:
- [Soviet Nixie Clock](https://youtu.be/)
- [VFD Modular Display](https://youtu.be/)
