A PCB doesn't have to do anything to be worth making.

These keychains are custom-designed printed circuit boards — real FR4 with copper traces, silkscreen, soldermask — shaped, finished, and put on a keyring. No components, no power, no function beyond looking exactly like what they are: a tiny piece of hardware you carry around.

## designs

Every keychain starts as a PCB layout. The silkscreen and copper layers become the art. The board shape is the canvas.

**Makers Colombia** — rectangular board with gear logo. One of the first runs, made for the Colombian maker community.

**DEF CON 2022, Las Vegas** — circular boards in black soldermask with gold ENIG finish. Skull and crossbones + event logo. Made for the DEF CON hacker conference. These showed what the circular form factor could do.

**UniAndinos** — custom run for Universidad de los Andes, Bogotá. Circular, black and gold, "#SoyEmprendedorUniandino". Client work — organization provides the design brief, I handle fabrication.

**DeepSea Developments** — octopus logo, circular. Internal / studio keychains.

## the gold version

The standout finish is ENIG (Electroless Nickel Immersion Gold) on black soldermask. The gold traces pop hard against the black. It's the same finish used on high-reliability PCBs — here it just makes them look good.

Available colors (soldermask): Black, Green, Purple, Red, Yellow, White, Blue.

## how they're made

Designed in KiCad. Fabricated at JLCPCB or PCBWay depending on the run. Circular boards need a custom board outline in the design — the fab cuts to shape. Minimum order quantities mean most runs are 20–50 units.
